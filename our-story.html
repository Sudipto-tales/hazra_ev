Hazra Electrical Bike

Our Story

One frame, one battery,
one stubborn idea.

The story of Hazra Electrical Bike is the story of builders who believed that electric scooters should feel like something riders actually want to ride.

Journey

Milestones that shaped us

2013

The Beginning

The first frame was pressed in a single piece. No screws to rattle, no joints to fail. Pure elegance.

2016

Swappable Battery

We introduced the first field-swappable pack. Riders could choose their battery without choosing their scooter.

2019

National Network

From 3 dealers to 48. We stopped being a prototype and became a service network that riders trust.

2022

26,000 Riders

Twenty-six thousand scooters on Indian roads. Every rider chose us because we built something worth choosing.

2025

Going Global

The same ethos that works in India is carrying us into Southeast Asia. Still one frame. Still one idea.
