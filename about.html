historyThe journey

How we got here

2017 — THE BEGINNING

A workshop and one bad prototype

Four engineers, 1,200 sq ft off Hazra Road, and a scooter that would not clear 40 km on a charge. We rebuilt the pack eleven times before it held 70.

2019 — FIRST HUNDRED

The Neo goes on sale

One hundred units, three dealers, all in West Bengal. Forty-one of those original scooters are still registered and running today.

2021 — NEW HORIZONS

Out of the state

Assembly moves to a 42,000 sq ft plant in Dankuni. Dealers open in Odisha, Bihar and Assam. The Urja platform replaces the first generation.

2023 — CONNECTED

Telematics as standard

Every scooter ships with a tracker and the Hazra EV app. The Cargo 7 launches for delivery fleets on the same platform.

2026 — TODAY

Nineteen states, 212 service points

The Vega 1000 takes us into the high-speed class. 91% of parts are now sourced inside India.

Vision & Mission

visibility

Vision

That by 2032 a family in a tier-3 Indian town buys an electric two-wheeler without thinking of it as a compromise — same range confidence, same service confidence, lower running cost. Nothing else about the decision should feel different.

target

Mission

Build vehicles that survive Indian roads, price them so a salaried rider can afford one, and put a service point within 30 km of every customer we sell to. In that order, and we will not sell into a district before the third one is true.

Quality over launch date

The Vega 1000 slipped two quarters because the rear suspension failed our own pothole loop. We shipped it late rather than shipped it soft.

Sustainable end to end

Every pack we sell comes back to us. We have recovered 9,100 batteries so far and route all of them through a certified recycler.
