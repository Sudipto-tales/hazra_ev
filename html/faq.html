Five habits that add two years to your pack

1 · Charge to 80% on ordinary days

Full 100% charges are for days you actually need the range. Partial cycles are gentler on cells.

2 · Do not park it flat

Leaving a pack below 20% for weeks is the single fastest way to lose capacity.

3 · Let it cool first

After a long ride, wait 20 minutes before plugging in. Charging a hot pack ages it faster.

4 · Use the supplied charger

Third-party fast chargers void the warranty and rarely respect our BMS cut-off.

5 · Monthly full cycle

One 100% charge a month lets the BMS recalibrate, so the range estimate on the app stays honest.
