01 Getting in

Signing in & your first password

The admin panel is the control room for the website and the field team. It runs in a browser at /admin — there is nothing to install. Accounts are created for you; you never sign up yourself.

Sign in

  1. Open /admin. If you are not signed in you land on the sign-in card, headed “The control panel for this website.”
  2. Enter your Email and Password. Tick Keep me signed in on this device only on a machine that is yours.
  3. Tap Sign in. A wrong address or password is reported right under the button as “Email or password is incorrect.”
Only an admin account can enter here. An employee (field) login is refused with “Admin role required”, and a deactivated account with “This account is deactivated.”

Forced first-login password change

When an admin creates your account — or resets a forgotten password — the system issues a temporary password and flags the account must_change_password. That flag is the panel asking you to set a password only you know before you do anything else.

  1. Sign in with the temporary password you were given.
  2. You are sent straight to Change password. Enter the temporary password as your current one, then a new password and confirm it.
  3. Save. The temporary credential stops working and the must_change_password flag clears — you will not be asked again.
Temporary passwords are shown to the admin who created the account exactly once and are never stored in readable form. If one is lost it is reset, not recovered — see section 3.
02 Your home screen

Dashboard & the live team map

After sign-in you land on the Dashboard. It opens with a greeting, a row of totals across your EV business, and a lead inbox — then the live team surfaces tell you who is out there right now.

Read the dashboard

  • Totals strip — counts of products, blogs, news, gallery items, open jobs, and incoming leads (test drive, dealership, contact, career).
  • Lead overview — a single figure for incoming leads, broken down by source, each row a link into that inbox.
  • Quick actions — jump straight to add a product, write a blog, post a job or open the lead inbox.

Watch the team live

  1. Open the team roster. Each member shows a live status — working, idle, offline or location unavailable — with their last GPS fix and how long they have been active.
  2. The totals above the roster count who is present, working, idle and offline, plus total distance, visits and reports for the chosen date.
  3. The view is live: positions and statuses stream in and update on their own. A long, stationary stop is flagged so you can check in.
The live feed updates itself every few seconds and reconnects automatically if the stream drops. If a connection can't be held, the panel quietly falls back to polling — you still see current positions, just refreshed a little less often.
Pick a date to review any past day. Totals, statuses and the map all follow the date you choose.
03 Your people

Employees — add, reset, deactivate

The Employees screen is the roster. Search it by name, employee code, region or department, and filter to active accounts only. From here you add new field staff, reset passwords and deactivate anyone who has left.

Add an employee

  1. Tap Add employee and fill in the required fields: name (at least 3 characters), a valid email, an employee code in the form EMP-1042, and a joining date that is not in the future.
  2. Optionally set phone, designation, department, region, reporting manager, blood group and address.
  3. Either type a starting password, or leave it blank and let the panel generate one.
  4. Save. If you let the panel generate the password, a temporary password is shown once on the confirmation — copy it and pass it on. The account opens flagged for a forced password change (section 1).
Email and employee code are unique per organisation. A duplicate is refused with “That email is already registered” or “That employee code is already in use.”

Reset a password

  1. Open the employee and choose Reset password.
  2. Type a new password, or leave it blank to have one generated. A generated one is shown once and arrives flagged for a forced change.
  3. Resetting signs the employee out of every device — that is the point of a reset. Hand them the new credential and they sign in fresh.

Deactivate an account

  1. Open the employee and switch Active off.
  2. They can no longer sign in, and they drop out of the live alert counts — but they stay in the roster and keep their history, so past routes and reports are never lost.
  3. Switch Active back on at any time to restore access.
Deactivating is reversible and keeps the record. A full account deletion is a separate, heavier path — a deleted account cannot be edited or reactivated.
04 Where they went

Route history & stops

Open any employee on any date to see the day drawn on a map: the path they travelled, every stop they made, and every company visit detected along the way.

  • The route — the matched track for the day. A closed day shows the finished, road-snapped path; today's open day draws live from the fixes that have arrived so far.
  • Stops — each place the employee stayed put long enough to count, with arrival and departure times. An open stop (still there) shows how long it has run.
  • Visits — stops matched to a customer location, which is what links a stop to a report.
Routes for the whole team on a date can be loaded together, or narrowed to specific people. Pick a date to step back through history.
What counts as a stop — the radius, how long is "stopped", and when a stop becomes a long stop — all come from the tracking rules in section 7. Change a rule and today's open day re-derives against it immediately; closed historical days are left exactly as they were.
05 Checking the work

Report review — approve or send back

Every visit report an employee submits lands here for review. Filter by decision —pending, approved or rejected — and work through the queue. The sidebar badge counts how many are still waiting.

  1. Open a report. You see the shop, the write-up, attached photos, any products sold and payment collected, and the session, GPS location and timestamp the app attached automatically.
  2. To accept it, choose Approve. To return it for a fix, choose Send back and add a note saying what needs changing.
  3. The decision and note are recorded with your name and the time, and the pending count drops by one.
A decision can be changed later — moving a report back to pending clears the review time so it re-enters the queue. Every change is kept in the audit trail.
Always leave a note when you send a report back — it is the one thing the employee sees explaining why, so make it specific.
06 The storefront

Product catalog management

The Products screen manages the EV scooters shown on the public site — their names, model codes, specifications, featured status and hero images. Search by name or model code and filter by whether each is published or hidden.

  1. Tap Add Product to create one, or open an existing row to edit it.
  2. Fill in the product's name, model code, category and brand, its specifications, and upload hero images.
  3. Set its visibility to Published to show it on the site, or Hidden to keep it off. Mark a model featured to surface it on the home page.
  4. Save. Use View on site to open the live products page in a new tab and confirm it looks right.
Hero images drive the look of the product page — upload clean, correctly-oriented photos. The same catalog feeds both the website and the field app's product picker, so a model you publish here is what employees select when logging a sale.
07 How tracking behaves

Tracking rules

One screen controls how the field app records location and how the server reads it back — for the whole organisation, not per employee. These are the knobs behind every status, stop and distance figure you see elsewhere in the panel.

What you can tune

  • Location interval — how often the app samples a fix (5–600 seconds).
  • Minimum accuracy — the metre threshold below which a fix is treated as too rough.
  • Stop radius & stop threshold — how close counts as "the same place", and how many minutes stationary makes it a stop.
  • Long-stop threshold — minutes before a stop is flagged as a long stop; must be at least the stop threshold.
  • Movement speed threshold — the speed above which someone counts as moving rather than idle.
  • Max jump — a speed ceiling that rejects impossible GPS leaps.
  • Offline & location-unavailable thresholds — how long without data before a member reads offline or location unavailable.
  • Sync batch size — how many queued fixes the app uploads per batch.
  1. Edit the values you want to change and save.
  2. The change applies read-time: stored fixes are never rewritten, but today's open day and same-day sessions are re-derived immediately against the new rules.
  3. Closed historical days are deliberately left untouched, so last month's calendar never silently changes under you. Every edit is versioned and kept in history.
These are powerful. The panel guards the obvious mistakes — long-stop must be ≥ stop threshold and interval must be 5–600 seconds — and rejects anything non-numeric. Change one knob at a time and watch the live roster to see its effect.
08 Reach & presence

Messaging & dealer locations

Two screens cover how you reach people and where you appear on the map: Message Delivery tracks every WhatsApp and SMS send, and Dealer Locations curates the public showroom map.

Message delivery

  1. Open Message Delivery to see WhatsApp and SMS sends. Filter by channel, status and date, or search by phone, purpose or reference.
  2. Each row shows its state: Sending, Accepted by provider, Sent, Delivered, Read, Failed, or Development log only.
  3. For a message that failed, use Retry to send it again. The list refreshes on its own so statuses stay current.
Accepted means the provider received the request; Delivered needs a delivery report back. Development logs record what would have been sent without sending anything.

Dealer locations

  1. Open Dealer Locations. Tap Add location and search a place, click the map, or drag the pin to the showroom entrance. Search data comes from Google Maps.
  2. Fill in the dealer name, phone, type (showroom, service centre, or both), full address, PIN code and opening hours. Verify the auto-filled address before saving.
  3. Set Visibility: Published shows it on the public map; Draft and Inactive stay hidden. Publishing requires a complete address, phone and map coordinates.
Use Check directions to open the pin in Google Maps and confirm it lands on the right doorway before you publish.
09 When something's off

Troubleshooting & FAQ

Quick answers to the things that come up most when running the panel.

I can't sign in
Check the address and password first. If it says “Admin role required,” you are using a field (employee) account — admins need an admin account. “This account is deactivated” means an administrator must switch it back on. Forgotten passwords are reset by another admin, not recovered.
A new employee can't log into the app
New accounts open with a temporary password and a forced password change. Make sure you passed on the exact temporary password shown once when you created them — if it was missed, open the employee and Reset password to issue a fresh one.
The temporary password disappeared before I copied it
It is shown once and never stored in readable form, so it cannot be looked up again. Open the employee and Reset password — that issues a new temporary credential and signs them out everywhere.
The live map looks frozen
The feed reconnects on its own, and falls back to polling if a live stream can't be held — so positions still refresh, just a little less often. If it still looks stale, the employee may simply be offline or have location unavailable; check their status and last fix. Reloading the page restarts the feed.
Someone shows a very long stop
A long stop is any stop past the long-stop threshold in Tracking rules (section 7). It is a prompt to check in, not an error — they may be in a genuine long meeting. If long stops fire too often, raise the threshold.
Changing a tracking rule didn't update old days
That is by design. Rule changes re-derive today's open day and same-day sessions immediately, but closed historical days are left untouched so past calendars never change retroactively. Stored fixes are never rewritten either.
A message failed to send
Open Message Delivery, filter to Failed, and use Retry on the row. Remember Accepted only means the provider took the request; Delivered needs a delivery report, and development logs never actually send.
A dealer won't publish
Publishing needs a complete address, a phone number and map coordinates. Fill any that are missing, drop the pin on the map, then switch Visibility to Published. Drafts and inactive locations stay hidden from visitors.

That's the whole panel. Keep this page handy — it updates as the admin tools do.
