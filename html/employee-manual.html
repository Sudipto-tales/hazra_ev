01 Getting on board

Install & sign in

The field app runs on Android. Install it, then sign in with the email and password your admin set up for you — there is nothing to create yourself.

Install the app

  1. Open the download page on your phone and tap to get the latest build.
  2. If Android asks, allow installs from this source, then open the app once it finishes.
  3. The first screen is Field Tracker — the sign-in chooser.

Employee vs Restricted

Two cards appear before any password is asked for. Pick the one that matches your account:

  • Employee Login — this is you. Start your day, log company visits and submit reports.
  • Restricted — marked Authorized Personnel Only. This is the admin surface (team monitoring, route history, report review). If you are not an admin, you do not use it.
Both cards use the same login. If you sign in on the Employee form with an admin account, the app stops you and says to use Restricted instead.

Sign in

  1. Tap Employee Login.
  2. Type your Email. The field shows a placeholder hint (you@company.com) — nothing is pre-filled, so type your own address in full.
  3. Type your Password. Tap the eye icon to check it before you submit.
  4. Tap Sign in. A wrong email or password is reported right under the button — correct it and try again.
Forgot your password? There is a Forgot password? link, but passwords cannot be looked up — ask your admin to reset it for you.
02 Before you set off

Permissions & Start Day

The app tracks your route only while your day is open, and only if it has location permission. Grant it once, then starting the day is a single tap.

Grant location

  1. When prompted, allow location access. Choose Allow all the time (background) so the route keeps recording when your screen is off or you are in another app.
  2. Keep your phone's GPS / location switch on. If it is off, the app cannot start your day and will offer to open settings for you.
  3. On Android 13 and newer you may also be asked to allow notifications — allow it so work reminders and (soon) the live session notification can reach you.
If location is blocked or set to "only while using the app", the Start Day button is held back and a banner on Home explains exactly what to fix. Tap Details to open the tracking status and Fix now.

Start your day

  1. On Home, find the big coloured session card at the top. Before you start it reads Ready.
  2. Tap Start Day. The card turns live, a timer begins counting, and you see “Day started at HH:MM · tracking is on”.
  3. From now on your joining time, working time, distance and sessions fill in automatically under Today's summary.
Start Day can be held back for three honest reasons: location is off (enable it), you are offline (the day must open against the server), or today is already closed by an admin. Each shows its own short dialog.
03 From the status bar Coming soon

The work-session notification

This feature is not built yet. It is planned for the Android app and this section describes how it will work once it ships — so you know what to expect. Nothing here is on your phone today.

What it will do

From the moment you Start Day until you End Day, a single notification will sit in your status bar showing your live work status. It will survive switching apps, locking the screen and even swiping it away, so you can run Break, Resume and End Day without opening the app first.

The three states

  • Working — shows the time elapsed since Start Day. Buttons: Break and End Day.
  • On break — shows how long you have been paused. Buttons: Resume and End Day.
  • Offline — fixes queued — shown when there is no signal; your points are still being saved. Resume / Break and End Day stay available.

What the buttons will do

  • Break pauses the current session.
  • Resume starts a fresh session and resumes tracking.
  • End Day opens the normal end-of-day closeout — it is never a one-tap finish, because ending a day is a reviewed step (see section 6).
It is designed to add no extra battery drain — it rides the tracking that is already running and updates only when your status changes. Android only for now.
04 Your main job

Logging visits & submitting reports

As you travel, the app detects stops at customer locations and lists them under Company visits. A report is how you record what happened at a visit — with photos, the models you sold and the payment collected.

Start a report

  1. From Home, tap New report in the quick-actions row, or open a detected visit and tap Add report to pre-fill the shop.
  2. Under Where you visited, type the company / shop name. It does not have to be on any list — just type it. You can also tap a detected visit chip to link the report to the route map.
  3. Under Report, add a short title and details (what was discussed, what was ordered, what needs follow-up).
  4. Under Products & sale (optional), pick a category, select the models you sold, set units, and enter the payment received.
  5. Deal value & follow-up are optional — add a deal figure or a follow-up date if there is one.

Attach photos

  1. In the Report section tap Add photo.
  2. Choose Take a photo (camera) or Choose from gallery (you can pick several at once).
  3. Photos are JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 8 MB each. Anything too large is flagged right in the picker, before you submit.
  4. Tap Submit report. The report is filed first, then the photos upload. If any photo fails, the report is still safely filed and a Retry photos button sends just the missing ones.
Your session, current GPS location and timestamp are attached automatically — you never type them. When a report is linked to a detected visit it also appears on your route map.
Offline safe. With no signal, the report is stored as a draft on your phone and uploaded automatically when the connection returns — see section 7.
05 Stepping away

Breaks & pauses

Taking a break pauses tracking without ending your day. Lunch, a long wait, anything — pause it rather than letting it record you sitting still.

  1. On the live session card, tap Break.
  2. The current session closes and the card shows “On break · tracking paused”. You will see “Session closed · start a new one when you resume”.
  3. When you are moving again, tap Resume session. A fresh session starts and tracking continues.
Your day stays open across breaks. Each resume adds to the Sessions count on Home, and break time does not count as working time.
06 Wrapping up

End of day / closeout

Ending the day is a short, reviewed step. The app shows what it measured, you declare what the day was, and then today is closed.

  1. On the session card, tap End Day.
  2. The app checks you are online and have a valid GPS fix — a day cannot be closed offline or without location.
  3. The closeout sheet opens. The top block, “What was tracked”, shows your GPS distance, detected visits, worked time and sessions.
  4. Enter the total distance you travelled and the total visits you made (your own figures), give the day a star rating, and optionally tag anything that got in the way or leave feedback.
  5. Tap Close the day. A recap card then appears on Home showing what you reported next to what GPS tracked.
Closing is final. Submitting closes today for good — only your admin can reopen it. If your declared numbers are far from the GPS figures you'll get a gentle heads-up, but you can still submit: it is your declaration.
If GPS and internet both go dark, the app may auto-close your current session to save battery. Your day stays open — just start a new session when you are ready.
07 When the bars drop

Weak signal & offline

Field work means dead zones. The app is built for them — your route and reports are safe on the phone until signal returns.

  • Nothing is lost. Location fixes are queued on the device and upload automatically the moment you are back online.
  • Low-accuracy points are still kept. A weak fix is better than a gap, so they are recorded rather than dropped.
  • Reports wait too. A report filed offline is stored as a draft and sent when the connection returns.
  • Ending the day needs signal. Closeout checks for a connection and a GPS fix first — so you can only close out when you are back online.

Check the tracking status

  1. Tap the location line on the session card, or go to Settings → Location status.
  2. The Tracking status sheet shows your last fix and accuracy, plus the sync queue: connection, queued locations, failed uploads and when it last synced.
  3. If you want to push the queue immediately once you have signal, tap Sync now.
In Settings → Tracking you can turn on High accuracy mode (rejects very poor fixes) and Sync on mobile data (off means image uploads wait for Wi-Fi).
08 Your account

Profile, settings & changing your password

The Settings tab holds everything about your account, tracking preferences, notifications and the app's look.

What you can change

  • Account — edit your phone and photo. Your ID, designation and reporting line are shown under Account information but are managed by your admin.
  • Tracking — location status, high-accuracy mode, sync on mobile data, battery saver.
  • Notifications — turn report reminders, work & session reminders and system notifications on or off.
  • App — switch theme (light / dark / follow system) and language.

Change your password

  1. Go to Settings → Security → Change password.
  2. Enter your current password (this is what authorises the change), then a new password of at least 8 characters that differs from the old one, and confirm it.
  3. Tap Change password. On success, every other device is signed out and this one stays signed in.
Passwords cannot be looked up. If you have forgotten yours, ask your admin to reset it — then sign in and change it to something only you know.
09 When something's off

Troubleshooting & FAQ

Quick answers to the things that come up most. Still stuck? Use Settings → Support → Contact support.

Start Day won't work
It is held back for one of three reasons: location is off or set to "only while using the app" (enable it — Allow all the time), you are offline (the day opens against the server, so you need signal), or today is already closed by an admin. The banner on Home names the exact cause and offers a fix.
I can't end my day
Closeout needs both an internet connection and a valid GPS fix. Move somewhere with signal and location, then tap End Day again. Your day stays open in the meantime — nothing is lost.
My photos didn't upload
The report itself is always filed first, so it is safe. If photos fail, the success screen shows a Retry photos button that re-sends only the pictures that didn't make it — it never files the report twice.
My session closed by itself
If GPS and internet are both off, the app auto-closes the current session to save battery and shows a notice with the time. Your day is still open — just start a new session when you are ready.
The app says my day is locked
Once a day is closed (by you at closeout, or by an admin) it is locked. Only an admin can reopen it. If that's wrong, contact your admin.
I forgot my password
Passwords can't be recovered. Ask your admin to reset yours, sign in with the temporary one, then set a new password under Settings → Security → Change password.
Does tracking drain my battery?
Tracking only runs while your day is open. Turn on Battery saver in Settings → Tracking to sample your location less often, and take a Break whenever you'll be stationary for a while.
