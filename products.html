Graphene or lithium-ion?

Both carry the same four-year warranty. The difference is weight, charge speed and what you pay up front.

Heavier by roughly 9 kg and fixed to the frame, but ₹11,500 cheaper and more tolerant of deep discharge. Right for a scooter that lives in a garage and charges overnight.

Removable, about 12 kg, and charges roughly 25% faster. If you park on the street or live above the ground floor, this is the one to buy.
